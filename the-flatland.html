Field guide / Phase 2A

Survive. Listen. Discover.

WASD / arrows
Move through the world. Buildings and natural obstacles are solid.
Shift
Sprint while moving. Costs stamina and extra food and water. Exhausted legs need a short recovery.
E · hold
Gather from a nearby green berry patch or blue spring. Depleted sources replenish over time.
F / R
Use one packed food / water. These restore 38 food / 45 water, up to 100.
Space
Field rest: faster stamina recovery, 35% less food use and 28% less water use, but no healing. Refuge rest adds healing when both food and water exceed 30. Move to get up.
Pause menu
Manual save / load, autosave status, and new expedition. Continue resumes the newest valid save. Loading starts paused.
Hover / click
Observe a visible form or open a citizen’s conversation and notes. T speaks to the nearest citizen. Use Tab / Enter or keys 1–7 to choose a topic; Escape closes the panel.
Trust & sources
Introductions reveal claimed names, not private lives. Vary your topics and return on later days. Asking about family too soon can offend. Rumors can be false; claims are not proof.
P / Escape
Pause or resume. H opens this guide. Switching away automatically pauses.

Food and water slowly decrease. An empty meter costs health. One day lasts two minutes of unpaused play. Complete all three expedition goals, then keep exploring.

Refuges are dashed circles with diamond markers, also shown on the field map. Society may judge your outline. Social prejudice is not a measure of fitness or worth. There is no combat, marriage, reproduction or succession yet. Conversation does not pause survival; keep your supplies ready. The seed reproduces the starting world, not different player actions.
