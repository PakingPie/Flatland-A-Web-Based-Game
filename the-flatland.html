Relationships & family

Vary your conversations. Friendship and attraction are separate. Marriage requires mutual interest. Same-sex spouses share household support; joint biological births currently require a male and female spouse.

At a refuge, advance days. Children mature at 18 days old. Retire at 40 days old or choose an adult descendant upon death. No adult heir means the playable lineage ends. Daughters can inherit too. Children look tiny until day 6, teenagers are smaller until day 18, adults reach full size, and elders shrink slightly from day 65. These size stages are visual; movement and interaction reach stay the same. Gold halos and gold minimap dots mark known family, including your spouse. Undiscovered relatives are not marked.

Survival & time

Food and water fill your pack; use them to refill your meters. Empty meters cost health. Field rest restores stamina; refuge rest also heals when food and water exceed 30.

Citizen menus and Family pause the world. Walking and interactions resume it. One day lasts 120 simulated seconds and adds one day of age. Refuge day advances provide subsistence and process aging and deaths.

Notes & saves

Details holds your notebook. Unconfirmed claims and rumors can be wrong. Marriage does not reveal hidden ancestry or a daughter's latent geometry.

Pause to save, load or export a backup. Browser saves stay in this browser and origin. A new life preserves the manual slot. Import validates before replacing active progress.
